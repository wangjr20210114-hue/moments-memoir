# Editorial method

## Build the factual spine

Use dates, original text, images, likes, comments, links and coverage metadata. Group adjacent posts into one period when they describe the same event, trip, project, relationship or emotional transition.

Separate explicit facts from the archive, user-supplied context, reasonable inference from sequence/text/images, and creative interpretation used only for tone or visuals.

Never turn a likely inference into a precise factual claim. When uncertainty affects the story, ask one focused question and continue independent work elsewhere.

## Select rather than transcribe

Keep posts that reveal at least one of these:

- a decision, transition, achievement, setback or recovery;
- a relationship or interaction that changes the meaning of an event;
- a representative detail of ordinary life;
- a trip, place, work, project, hobby or creative piece central to the period;
- a recurring personal theme supported across multiple posts.

Usually remove advertising, repeated reposts, empty check-ins, duplicate media and context-free links. Do not equate low engagement with low meaning.

## Use interactions carefully

Likes can show scale or support, but counts should not replace narrative. Quote comments only when they add context, humor or relationship meaning. Follow the user's name policy; relation labels and nicknames are the safest default.

## Optional motifs

Detect poetry, music, books, food, exercise, games, pets, travel, family, study or work as possible recurring motifs. Use them only when they genuinely shape the archive or the user selects them. Poetry extraction is optional.

## Write the memoir

Prefer scene, date, decision and consequence. Combine the author's original wording with concise narration. Avoid generic praise and fabricated interior thoughts. A strong chapter usually contains a concrete opening, what changed and what it cost, one memorable detail or interaction, an original sentence/comment/artifact when useful, and a transition to the next period.

Vary chapter length according to significance. Preserve the user's voice, including humor and ordinary phrasing, rather than rewriting every post into solemn prose.
