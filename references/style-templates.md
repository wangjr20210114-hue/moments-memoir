# Style templates

Offer these as a short menu when the user has no style reference. A free-form description or supplied reference takes precedence. Mix at most two presets unless the user asks for a collage.

## A. Cinematic journey

- Deep background, luminous accents, large chapter numbers and full-bleed images.
- Best for long growth arcs, study, career, travel and major transitions.
- Motion: restrained parallax, sticky chapters and fades.

## B. Warm scrapbook

- Paper texture, handwritten accents, taped photos and warm muted colors.
- Best for family, friendship, childhood and everyday life.
- Motion: page turns, gentle image reveals and optional audio postcards.

## C. Editorial magazine

- Strong typography, asymmetric grids, captions and generous white space.
- Best for creative portfolios, academic work and a photo-rich yearbook.
- Motion: minimal; prioritize reading and image rhythm.

## D. Minimal timeline

- Calm monochrome palette, compact milestones and accessible navigation.
- Best for large archives, professional summaries and users who want clarity.
- Motion: almost none; excellent for print/PDF conversion.

## E. Travel atlas

- Route lines, map-like sections, city colors and grouped travel periods.
- Best when locations and journeys are the main narrative device.
- Only show precise locations when the user permits it.

## F. Graphic comic

- Illustrated protagonist, panels, speech bubbles and bold color blocks.
- Best for humorous archives, campus memories and family-friendly storytelling.
- Other people can use generic characters when the user does not want likenesses.

## Custom style brief

Translate a free-form request into:

1. three visual adjectives;
2. color and contrast direction;
3. typography character;
4. layout rhythm;
5. motion level;
6. original-photo versus illustration balance;
7. privacy treatment for other people.

Ask for clarification only when two interpretations would materially change the result. Otherwise create a first reviewable screen and refine from concrete feedback.
